04 / Before we start

Good questions.

How much will my project cost?

Each proposal is priced around the finished product, its scope and the value of solving the problem. Tell us who the software is for, what it needs to do and any budget or timing constraints. We can then discuss a concrete scope and price.

Do you bill by the hour?

Our commercial model is based on the agreed product and delivered value. The proposal defines the price and payment schedule for that scope.

What counts as finished?

We agree on acceptance criteria before implementation. These describe what the software must do and how you will verify it, along with the documentation and handover included in the delivery.

Can you work on our existing software?

Yes. We first review the existing system and the problem you want to solve, then define a bounded piece of work with clear acceptance criteria.

What happens after delivery?

We agree on the handover and any included support in the contract. Ongoing maintenance and future improvements can be scoped separately.
